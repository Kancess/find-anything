## Consumer protection
- Dealer statutory warranty reported as **3 months or 5,000 km for cars < 10 years old and < 160,000 km**; cars outside that must be roadworthy at sale (source was a motor-trade page citing the old Act – **verify with NSW Fair Trading**). Cooling-off applies to some licensed-dealer sales; private sales have none.

## Novice-driver rule that changes what you can buy
- **P1/P2 licence holders cannot drive "prohibited" high-performance vehicles** (power-to-tare-mass ratio over 130 kW/tonne, plus other listed types such as many turbo/V8 models). **Check the NSW government prohibited-vehicle search for the exact make/model/variant before buying.** Source: nsw.gov.au P1 and P2 prohibited vehicles.
